# Workload expansion: weeks 1–2

All ten units retain their beginner baseline and existing URLs, practical data,
lecture/practical pairing, sidebar scaffold, and biological examples. The new
work develops explanation, verification, independent transfer and writing. It
does not add a programming-language survey or a chemistry prerequisite.

Each daily page now has a substantial **Extended practical** and **Independent
transfer** block. They are core work, not optional stretch tails, and include
prediction, checking, interpretation and saved outputs. No duration is stated:
how long these take beginners is unknown until piloted. A context choice still means one chosen route, not compulsory completion
of all biology and chemistry alternatives.

## Observable expansion, not a claim about study time

- Lecture content: **79 → 160 slides** (2.03×), plus one title slide per deck.
- Image-bearing content slides: **40 → 80**; half the expanded content slides use diagrams or actual-data plots.
- Practical source words: **10,533 → 18,847** (1.79×; includes code/labels).
- Explicit numbered practical actions: **61 → 180**. Counts describe source structure, not difficulty or a grading rule.
- Weekly quizzes: **6 → 12 questions each**, with worked explanations.
- New assets: 40 diagrams/plots, generated by `make-lecture-figures-weeks1-2.py`.

The extra lecture material uses demonstrations of stale assignments, mismatched
observations, file identity versus location, archive snapshots, wrong-interpreter
recovery, units, impossible requests and narrative/source consistency. Day 8 also
now supplies the plot/table objects explicitly before its export example, rather
than relying on objects created only in the practical.

## Verified numerical anchors

- Plant transfer: 18 rows, 4 input columns, 18 unique IDs, 9 plants per group, no
  missing input measurements. T01 grew 15.0 mm; T18 is treated, 21.6 → 43.0 mm.
- Stale result: 20 → 37 gives 17 mm; changing final to 39 leaves stored 17 until
  recalculation yields 19. Wrong positional vector pairing produces 14, 15, 21 mm.
- Supplied solution cases give 50, 100, 150 mmol/L for each solute. The original
  individual NaCl/glucose cases both give 50 mmol/L; doubled mass gives 100 and
  doubled final volume gives 25. All formulas/constants are given to students.
- D01–D03 need 2.5, 5, 10 mL stock. D04 calculates 100 mL for 50 mL final total and is
  flagged impossible by dilution, rather than deleted or reported as a recipe.
- Mean of 10, NA, 20 is unavailable unless omission is explicit; available-value
  mean 15 uses 2 observations. Recording a real zero instead gives mean 10 from 3.
